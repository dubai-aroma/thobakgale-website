    Before you call

    The questions people ask first.

    Can you promise I will win?

    No honest attorney can. What we promise is the truth at the start, a clear plan, and a director who owns your matter from the first call.

    I have been served. What do I do right now?

    Do not ignore it and do not reply to the other side yourself. Check the date of service, keep every page, and call us. For most summonses you have about 10 court days to give notice that you will defend.

    How quickly can you act on an urgent matter?

    Tick the urgent box in the form or phone the office. Urgent matters, such as court papers, a short deadline or a closing date, are triaged by a director straight away.

    How will I know what it costs?

    After the assessment you receive the proposed scope and fee basis in writing before work is billed. Where court is likely to be slow or costly, we will say so and propose mediation or arbitration if that serves you better.

    Do you handle family law and personal injury?

    Yes. Our litigation practice includes divorce, custody and maintenance matters, personal injury claims and delictual disputes, alongside commercial work.

    Do you work with start ups?

    Yes. Start up counselling includes a free initial consultation, and many young companies retain us as their outsourced in house counsel.

    Do you act on matters outside South Africa?

    Our founding director is Secretary of the Savannah Law Network, whose members span more than 20 African countries, so cross border matters start with a relationship that already exists.

    Is my enquiry confidential?

    Yes. Enquiries are treated as confidential and handled in line with POPIA.
