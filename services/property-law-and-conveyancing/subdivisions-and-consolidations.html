Before you call

Questions people ask first.

Do I need municipal approval first?

Yes. The planning approval and surveyor's diagrams come first, then registration in the Deeds Office.

Can I sell part of my property?

Once the subdivision is approved and registered, the new portion can be transferred.

What happens to my bond?

The bank must consent, and the bond may need to be amended or cancelled.
