Before you call

Questions people ask first.

How long does a transfer take?

Transfers commonly take a few months, depending on bonds, clearances and the Deeds Office. We give you a realistic timeline at the start.

Who chooses the transfer attorney?

Usually the seller appoints the transferring attorney, but this can be agreed in the offer to purchase.

What costs should I expect?

Transfer costs, transfer duty where applicable, and Deeds Office fees. We give you a written estimate at the start.
