Before you call

Questions people ask first.

When can erven be sold?

Erven can be sold earlier subject to conditions, but transfer only happens once the township register is opened.

What stops a township register opening?

Usually unfulfilled conditions of establishment, missing documents or plan issues. We check this early.

Do you handle the transfers afterwards?

Yes. We handle the individual and bulk transfers and bonds.
