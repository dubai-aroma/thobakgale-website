Before you call

Questions people ask first.

Is an offer to purchase binding once signed?

Yes. Once accepted it is a binding agreement of sale, subject to its conditions. Read it before you sign.

Can you review a lease before I sign?

Yes. We review leases for tenants and landlords and explain what to negotiate.

The builder is late. What can I do?

It depends on the building agreement. We review your rights to penalties, completion by others or cancellation.
