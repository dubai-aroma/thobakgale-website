Before you call

Questions people ask first.

What is a notarial bond?

A bond over movable property, registered in the Deeds Office, giving the lender security over assets like equipment or stock.

Can private lenders register a bond?

Yes. Private lenders can take a mortgage bond over property with the owner's consent.

What happens if the borrower defaults?

Security gives you a route to recover. We also handle enforcement and execution.
