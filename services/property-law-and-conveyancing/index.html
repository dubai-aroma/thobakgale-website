Before you call

Questions people ask first.

How long does a transfer take?

Transfers commonly take a few months from signature to registration, depending on bonds, clearances and the Deeds Office. We give you a realistic timeline at the start and update you at each step.

Do you act for developers?

Yes. We handle development agreements, subdivisions and consolidations, sectional title and township registers and bulk transfers.
