Questions people ask first.

Do you act for creditors or debtors?

Mainly for lenders, creditors and companies in distress who need a plan. We advise directors on their duties and their options.

Is business rescue the same as liquidation?

No. Business rescue aims to save the company or get a better result than liquidation, under a supervised plan and a pause on most creditor action.
