How long does debt recovery take?

If the debtor does not defend, judgment can come quickly. Defended matters take longer. We give you a realistic plan at the start.

What if the debtor has no money?

We check assets first, so you do not spend money chasing nothing.

Then the route is a claim in the insolvent estate. See creditor claims.
