Before you call

Questions people ask first.

What does proving a claim mean?

Submitting your claim with supporting evidence to the presiding officer at a meeting of creditors, so you can share in distributions and vote.

Can we apply to liquidate a debtor?

Yes, where the requirements are met. It is also powerful leverage in debt recovery.

Will I get paid?

It depends on the estate, your security and the other creditors. We give you a realistic view early.
