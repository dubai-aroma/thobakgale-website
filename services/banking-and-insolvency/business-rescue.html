6months: the distress test
The clock

Distress is a legal status. It comes with duties.

A company is financially distressed if it is reasonably unlikely to pay its debts within the next six months, or likely to become insolvent in that time. Once the board resolves to begin rescue, it has 5 business days to publish notice and appoint a practitioner.

Companies Act 71 of 2008, s128 and s129

A general guide. Court day counts skip weekends and public holidays but not court recess. Your documents set the exact dates.

Before you call

Questions people ask first.

What is business rescue?

A Companies Act process that places a financially distressed company under temporary supervision, with a moratorium on most creditor action, while a rescue plan is developed.

Are directors personally liable?

Directors can be liable for reckless trading and breaches of their duties. Taking advice early protects you.

We are a creditor of a company in rescue. What now?

You have rights to participate, vote on the plan and challenge irregularities. We represent creditors.
