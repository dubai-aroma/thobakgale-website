Before you call

Questions people ask first.

How long does registration take?

It varies and can take a long time at the registry. Filing early gives you priority from the filing date.

How long does a registration last?

A South African trademark registration lasts ten years and can be renewed.

Can I register a logo and a name?

Yes. Many brands register both, sometimes in more than one class.
