Before you call

Questions people ask first.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Start up counselling includes a free initial consultation. After that you receive the scope and fee basis in writing before any work is billed.

We already registered the company ourselves. Is it too late?

No. Many founders register first and come to us when the first investor, partner or key hire appears. We review what you have and fix what matters.

Do we need a shareholder agreement if we are friends?

Especially then. It is far easier to agree what happens if someone leaves while everyone is still friends.
