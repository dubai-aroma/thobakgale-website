Before you call

Questions people ask first.

Can we make everyone an independent contractor?

Only if the relationship really is one. Labour law looks at how the work is done, not what the contract calls it.

Are restraints of trade enforceable?

They can be, if they protect a legitimate interest and are reasonable in scope, area and time. We draft them to give you the best chance.

Can you help if we already have a dispute?

Yes. See our labour disputes page.
