30days to refer a dismissal dispute
The clock

Dismissed? Your 30 days have already started.

A dismissal dispute must generally be referred to the CCMA or a bargaining council within 30 days of the dismissal. Unfair labour practice disputes have 90 days. Enter your dismissal date.

Labour Relations Act, s191(1)

A general guide. Court day counts skip weekends and public holidays but not court recess. Your documents set the exact dates.

6weeks to review a CCMA award
The clock

An award went against you? Six weeks.

An application to review a CCMA arbitration award must generally be brought in the Labour Court within six weeks of the award being served. Enter the date it was served.

Labour Relations Act, s145(1)

A general guide. Court day counts skip weekends and public holidays but not court recess. Your documents set the exact dates.

Before you call

Questions people ask first.

What if the 30 days have passed?

You can apply for condonation, but it is not guaranteed and you must explain the delay. Speak to us immediately.

Can attorneys represent me at the CCMA?

At arbitration, legal representation depends on the type of dispute and the commissioner's discretion. We prepare you either way and represent you where allowed.

Do you act for employers and employees?

Yes. We act for either, never both sides of the same dispute.
