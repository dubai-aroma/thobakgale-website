Questions people ask first.

How long does commercial litigation take?

It depends on the court and whether the other side defends. Many matters settle once a strong case is on the table. We give you a realistic timeline at the start.

Can you promise I will win?

No honest attorney can. What we promise is the truth at the start, a clear plan and a director who owns your matter.

Is arbitration faster than court?

Often, and it is private. Where the contract allows it, or both sides agree, we will tell you whether it serves you better.
