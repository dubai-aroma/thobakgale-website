The clock

The law sets the process. We keep it moving.

Under the PIE Act an eviction needs a court order, and the occupiers must receive written notice of the hearing at least 14 days before it takes place.

PIE Act 19 of 1998, s4(2)

There is no single date to calculate here. The process sets the pace, and we keep it moving.

No. Self help evictions are unlawful. You need a court order.

How long does an eviction take?

It depends on the court, the occupiers and whether they oppose. We give you a realistic timeline at the start.

Can I claim the arrear rent too?

Yes. We can claim arrear rent and damages alongside or after the eviction.
