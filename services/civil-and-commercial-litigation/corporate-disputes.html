Before you call

Questions people ask first.

Can a court remove a director?

In certain circumstances the Companies Act allows a court to declare a director delinquent or under probation. We advise on whether that route fits.

Is there a deadline?

Not usually a fixed one, but delay favours whoever controls the company and its money. Move early.

Can this be settled privately?

Often. Mediation and negotiated buy outs keep the business alive and the dispute out of the public record.
