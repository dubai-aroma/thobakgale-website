The clock

Damages claims have an expiry date.

Most delictual claims prescribe three years after you knew, or should have known, of the harm and who caused it. Claims against the state also need written notice within six months.

Prescription Act, s11(d) and s12(3)

Before you call

Questions people ask first.

What is a delict?

A wrongful act that causes harm, such as negligence that damages your property or a false statement that harms your reputation. The person responsible can be liable for the loss.

Is there a time limit?

Most claims prescribe after three years. Claims against government bodies also need written notice within six months, so speak to us early.
